## 2. Voice Recordings & Audio Source

The audio voice recordings embedded and streamed by this application are sourced from the **Ministry of Education, Taiwan (中華民國教育部)**:

- **Source Database**: 《臺灣台語常用詞辭典》 (Dictionary of Common Taiwanese Hokkien, Ministry of Education, Taiwan).
- **Data Mirror & Hosting**: Shared via the [ChhoeTaigi (找台語)](https://github.com/ChhoeTaigi) project database mirrors.
- **License**: The original audio files and dictionary database are released under the [Government Website Open Information Announcement](https://www.moe.gov.tw/cp.aspx?n=AA9F19E92931CE1C) (政府網站資料開放宣告), which is compatible with the **Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 3.0 Taiwan (CC BY-ND 3.0 TW)** license.
