📖 How Taiwanese Tone Sandhi Works

在臺灣台語中，當兩個或更多音節組合成一個詞組（或句子）時，**除了最後一個音節維持本調外，前面的音節皆須變調**。這種語音現象稱為**連讀變調 (Tone Sandhi)**。

  • 二字詞組 (2-Syllable)：第 1 字變調，第 2 字維持本調（例如：驚人 kiann1+lang5 ➔ kiāⁿ-lâng）。
  • 三字詞組 (3-Syllable)：第 1、2 字變調，第 3 字維持本調（例如：台灣人 tai5+uan5+lang5 ➔ Tāi-uān-lâng）。若末字為輕聲，則倒數第二字讀本調，最前字變調。
  • 四字詞組 (4-Syllable)：依結構不同有兩種變調方式：
    1. *連續變調 (Sequential)*：前 3 字變調，第 4 字維持本調。
    2. *兩兩變調 (2+2 Split)*：常用于複合詞組（如「台灣大學」），拆為前二字一組（1變調 ➔ 2本調）與後二字一組（3變調 ➔ 4本調）。

🍃 Neutral Tone & Sandhi (輕聲與變調)

In Taiwanese Hokkien, a **neutral tone (輕聲)** is represented by a double hyphen -- before the syllable (e.g., khì--khì). Unlike standard sandhi:

  • Word 1 (Preceding word) does NOT change: Under this grammatical condition, Word 1 retains its original base tone (本調) instead of standard sandhi.
  • Word 2 is pronounced softly: It is spoken low and short. The trainer automatically softens and speeds up the audio for neutral tones.
  • Quick Type support: You can check the "Neutral Tone" box, or type -- before Word 2 (e.g. --khì or --khi3) to trigger the rule automatically.

🔄 Triplicate Sandhi (三字疊字變調 AAA)

In Taiwanese Hokkien, when an adjective is triplicated (AAA structure) for emphasis (e.g., âng-âng-âng 紅紅紅), a special sandhi rule applies:

  • Word 3 (Last word): retains its original base tone (本調).
  • Word 2 (Middle word): undergoes standard general tone sandhi (一般變調).
  • Word 1 (First word):
    • If the base tone is 1, 5, 7, or 8: It changes to a mid-rising tone (中升調, T5). For entering Tone 8 ending in -h, it also drops the glottal stop -h and becomes Tone 5.
    • If the base tone is 2, 3, or 4: It undergoes standard general tone sandhi (一般變調).
  • Auto-detection: The trainer automatically detects when you input three identical syllables in 3-Syllable Mode and applies this triplicate sandhi rule.
